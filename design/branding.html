06 / Construction How the lockup is built

Anatomy

Two italic stems - Finn and Est - set in Newsreader 500. After each stem, a smaller mono superscript (fi / et) coloured to its respective flag blue. The superscript reads as a lemma-tag, the same syntax the parser uses on every word.

Proportions

Superscript = 0.32em of wordmark size, baseline shifted via vertical-align: super. Letter-spacing tightens from −0.020em at small sizes to −0.035em at hero. Below 24px the superscripts are dropped - at favicon size, "FE" two-tone tile only.
