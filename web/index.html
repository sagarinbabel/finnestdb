How FinEstDB works

Most language apps make you study vocabulary in isolation. FinEstDB starts from the text you actually want to read, and builds your vocabulary outward from there.

1

Paste a text

Drop in a paragraph, an article, or a chapter in Finnish or Estonian.

2

Parse the words

We split the text into unique lemmas, show dictionary details, and let you triage rows as known or ignored.

3

Save a deck

Turn the parsed vocabulary into review cards tied back to the source text you pasted.

4

Track progress over time

Your known-word and due-review counts update as you review and mark cards known.
