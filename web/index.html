How FinEstDB works

Most language apps make you study vocabulary in isolation. FinEstDB starts from the text you actually want to read, and builds your vocabulary outward from there.

1

Paste a text

Drop in a paragraph, an article, or a chapter in Finnish or Estonian.

2

See your gap

We split the text into words and show which ones you already know, which are new, and which carry the meaning.

3

Unlock the right words

Learn the small set of words that turns a confusing text into one you can read, and watch your comprehension climb.

4

Track progress over time

Your known-word count grows as you learn. Come back to a text you couldn't read last month and see what changed.
