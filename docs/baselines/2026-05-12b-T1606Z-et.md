**Still incorrect** (carried from 2026-05-12a, not regressed nor fixed):

- `Eestis` → Eesti/NOUN (gold says PROPN). Both PROPN and NOUN rows are now in the dict at equal priority; PROPN vs NOUN tiebreak needs a separate fix.
- `õuna` → õud/NOUN (gold says õun/NOUN). Different-lemma noun ambiguity; the dict's `õud` and `õun` rows compete on equal footing without context.
- `Koer`/`Koerad`/`koer` → koer/ADJ (gold says NOUN). Adjective-vs-noun homograph at equal priority; separate from the bias scope here.

## Open items this baseline surfaces (carried for follow-up)

1. **PROPN vs NOUN tiebreak** (`Eestis`, future capitalized-PROPN homographs). Picking by alphabetical POS is fragile when both readings exist.
2. **Different-lemma NOUN ambiguity** (`õuna` between õud and õun). Needs context or frequency signal — current ranking treats both as equal.
3. **ADJ vs NOUN homograph** (`koer`, `Koer`, `Koerad`). Same shape of problem, different POS pair.
