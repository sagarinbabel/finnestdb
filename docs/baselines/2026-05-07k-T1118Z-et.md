## Net effect vs `2026-05-07j` (custom parser, FEATS data-thread + Ekilex bulk drop)

This is the headline movement of the FEATS migration on ET — the lift

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| et-manual | **+22.2** | **+22.2** | **+83.3** | **+66.7** | +8.3 |

**Reading**

- **et-grammar grammar accuracy soared from 19.6% → 78.4%** (+58.8pp). LEARNINGS.md projected ~95%; we landed at 78.4. The remaining ~16pp gap to estnltk's 94.1% is on Vabamorf-disambiguated cases (multi-lemma homonyms like `naeris` NOUN-vs-VERB, `keelt` etc.) where Ekilex's morph_code carries one reading per `(lemma, pos)` while estnltk picks per-occurrence using contextual disambiguation.
- **et-manual fully closed lemma+POS to 100/100**, with grammar now at 83.3% (was 0%). Full% landed at 77.8% (was 11.1%). The bulk-Ekilex coverage closed the long-tail forms that the kaikki+public-headwords slice was missing.
- **Slight regressions on et-grammar lemma (88.6 → 86.7) and POS (96.2 → 91.4)** — Ekilex bulk introduces multi-lemma homonyms (e.g. PROPN/NOUN tied surfaces) that the dict ranker mishandles. Same kind of issue [`b327d4f`](https://github.com/sagarinbabel/finnestdb/commit/b327d4f) (PR 0.5, "Source-aware lookup recovery") fixed for the public-headwords slice; recurs at bulk scale. **Action:** the source-priority + case-match heuristics in `pickBestFormCandidate` need re-validation against the Ekilex bulk shape; expect a follow-up ranker tweak to recover the lost lemma/POS pp without regressing grammar.

## Reproduction

```
# Bootstrap Ekilex bulk drop (one-time; raw is already cached locally)
make reduce-ekilex                # raw → definitions/forms shards
make import-ekilex-details-et     # shards → DB

# Run eval
make parser
export DYLD_LIBRARY_PATH="$(pwd)/parser/target/release:$DYLD_LIBRARY_PATH"
make setup-estnltk
bash scripts/parser-comparison-et.sh
```

ET UD treebanks (CC BY-NC-SA, gitignored under `localdata/parser-eval/et/gold/`)
are not present in this measurement. With `make import-ud-gold-et` populating
that directory, the comparison script auto-discovers and runs them too.
